# Game Brief
Title: Hex Hollow
Pitch: A young witch defends her village wells from cursed spirits.
Genre: third-person action adventure
References: Spyro (feel only)
Art style: stylized dark fairytale
Theme/setting: haunted forest village at night
Mood: eerie but cozy
Palette: deep purples, moss green, warm lantern orange
Camera: third-person

Premise: Spirits poisoned the village wells on the night of the blood moon.
Protagonist: Mira, an apprentice witch
Goal: purify the three wells before dawn
Antagonist: the Hollow King
Ending: dawn breaks and the village wakes
Tone: playful dread

Core loop: explore the village -> gather herbs -> brew potions -> fight spirits -> purify a well
Win: purify 3 wells
Lose: health reaches zero
Mechanics (must):
- melee spell attack
- herb gathering
- potion brewing at cauldrons
Mechanics (should): dodge roll
Progression: each purified well unlocks a stronger spell
Session length: 15 minutes

Platform: android
Orientation: landscape
Controls: virtual joystick + buttons (attack, brew, dodge)

Levels/areas: 3
World: forest path, village square, cursed well grove
World size: medium
Landmarks: old windmill, chapel ruin

Characters:
- Mira (player, idle/run/cast/hit/death)
- spirit (enemy, float/attack/hit/die)

Must-have assets: cauldron, herb bush, lantern, wooden house
Asset edits:
- water well -> witch well with mossy stones, glowing green water and hanging bones
Screens: main menu, HUD, pause, settings, game over, win
Music: soft music-box waltz
SFX: spell cast, potion bubble, spirit wail

V1 includes: 3 areas, 1 enemy type, 3 wells
V1 excludes: inventory screen, voice acting
Performance target: 60 fps on mid-range Android
